2. **Treasury Intelligence:** Many people confuse these two modules.  
* **Cash Flow Intelligence** answers **"How much cash do we have today and in the future?"**  
* **Treasury Management** answers **"How should we manage that cash to maximize liquidity, reduce risk, and improve returns?"**  
  1. **Bank Management:** It centralizes all banking operations. Instead of logging into multiple banking portals, treasury users can monitor and operate all accounts from a single dashboard.  
     1. **Multi-bank Connectivity:** The platform integrates with all supported banks and provides a unified interface.  
     2. **Bank Account Hierarchy:** Large developers often have accounts organized by business unit or project.  
     3. **Digital Signatures:** Large-value payments typically require digital authorization.  
     4. **Payment Approvals:** Organizations define approval rules. The system routes payments automatically based on these rules.  
     5. **Bank Statement Ingestion:** Every day, banks generate account statements. Instead of manually downloading and uploading them, the platform imports statements automatically. Typical data includes:  
        1. Credits  
        2. Debits  
        3. Interest  
        4. Bank charges  
        5. Returned payments  
        6. Closing balances  
     6. **API Integration:** Modern banks expose APIs for:  
        1. Balance inquiries  
        2. Transaction history  
        3. Payment initiation  
        4. Status tracking  
     7. **SWIFT Integration:** SWIFT is a global messaging network used for international financial transactions. Real estate companies with overseas investors, consultants, or suppliers may use SWIFT for cross-border payments.  
  2. **Liquidity Management:**   
     1. **Cash Pooling:** Rather than borrowing externally, treasury may pool available cash (subject to legal, tax, lender, and escrow restrictions) to improve enterprise liquidity. This can reduce interest costs and improve cash utilization.  
     2. **Cash Concentration:** Instead of maintaining small balances in many accounts, treasury can consolidate funds into a central account for better control and investment.  
     3. **Idle Cash Identification:** Idle cash earns little or no return. The AI identifies this and suggests the below. The recommendation depends on upcoming cash requirements and investment policies.:  
        1. Short-term fixed deposits  
        2. Liquid mutual funds  
        3. Early repayment of expensive debt  
     4. **Inter-company Funding:** Large groups often transfer funds between entities to support operations. The AI can recommend an inter-company loan if permitted by corporate policies, lender agreements, accounting standards, and applicable regulations. It also tracks:  
        1. Interest  
        2. Repayment schedule  
        3. Documentation  
        4. Compliance  
     5. **Notional Pooling:** Notional pooling is a banking arrangement where balances across multiple accounts are considered together for interest calculations without physically moving money. Instead of charging interest on the overdraft and paying low interest on deposits separately, the bank may calculate interest based on the combined net balance, depending on the banking arrangement. This can reduce financing costs while keeping funds in their original accounts.  
3. **Investment Management:** Treasury is responsible for investing surplus cash safely while maintaining sufficient liquidity. The goal is to earn a return without jeopardizing the company's ability to meet future obligations.  
   1. **Fixed Deposits (FDs):** The AI may recommend placing part of this amount in a fixed deposit after considering:  
      1. Forecast cash needs  
      2. Expected returns  
      3. Early withdrawal penalties  
      4. Investment policies  
   2. **Liquid Funds:** Liquid mutual funds provide quick access to money while typically offering returns higher than idle bank balances. They are suitable for short-term surplus cash that may be needed soon.  
   3. **Money market instruments**  
   4. **Government securities**  
   5. **Other approved short-term investments**  
   6. **Maturity Tracking:** Every investment has a maturity date. The AI alerts the treasury before maturity so funds can be reinvested or used for upcoming obligations.  
   7. **Investment Yield Optimization:** If two approved investments offer different expected returns for similar risk and liquidity profiles, the AI can recommend the option that better aligns with the company's investment policy and cash needs.  
   8. **FX (Foreign Exchange) Management:** FX (Foreign Exchange) Management helps companies manage transactions involving multiple currencies and protect themselves from exchange rate fluctuations. Even a small change in exchange rates can significantly impact project costs and profitability. Although many real estate developers operate primarily in India, larger firms often deal with:  
      1. Foreign investors  
      2. Overseas consultants and architects  
      3. Imported construction materials  
      4. International contractors  
      5. Foreign currency loans  
      6. **Forex Exposure:** Exposure is the amount of money that could be affected by currency movements. The system continuously monitors the below to calculate total exposure:  
         1. Open contracts  
         2. Outstanding invoices  
         3. Future payments  
         4. Future receipts  
      7. **Hedging:** Hedging reduces the risk of adverse exchange rate movements. The objective is not to generate profit but to reduce uncertainty. The company can lock in today's exchange rate through an approved hedging instrument, reducing the risk of future currency fluctuations. The Treasury Agent can identify exposures and suggest when hedging may be appropriate, subject to company policy and treasury approval.  
      8. **Forward Contracts:** A forward contract allows a company to agree today on the exchange rate for a future transaction. The company pays using the agreed forward rate, reducing the impact of market fluctuations. The platform tracks:  
         1. Contract value  
         2. Maturity date  
         3. Settlement status  
         4. Covered exposure  
      9. **Currency Gains/Losses:** Suppose the company has a USD receivable. The Treasury Dashboard reports realized and unrealized foreign exchange gains and losses to support financial reporting and decision-making.  
   9. **Treasury Dashboard:** The Treasury Dashboard provides a consolidated view of enterprise treasury health. Think of it as the command center for the CFO and Treasury team.  
      1. **Liquidity KPIs:** These metrics help determine whether the organization has sufficient liquidity to meet near-term obligations. Key indicators include:   
         1. Available Cash  
         2. Available Credit Lines  
         3. Short-term Obligations  
      2. **Bank Exposure:** Treasury tracks how much cash is held with each banking partner. If one bank holds a disproportionately large share of funds, the AI may recommend diversifying balances to reduce concentration risk.  
      3. **Credit Limits:** Companies often have sanctioned credit facilities. This helps the treasury understand available borrowing capacity before seeking additional financing.  
      4. **Cash Utilization:** The dashboard categorizes cash based on how it is being used. This gives management a clear picture of where cash is allocated.  
      5. **Treasury Health Score:** The AI calculates an overall health score by considering multiple factors, such as the below. If conditions worsen, the score decreases and the AI explains the contributing factors:   
         1. Liquidity position  
         2. Cash concentration  
         3. Credit utilization  
         4. Idle cash  
         5. Investment performance  
         6. Debt maturity profile  
         7. FX exposure  
         8. Upcoming obligations  
      6. **AI Tasks:** Treasury Management is not just about displaying data. The AI actively recommends actions.  
         1. **Optimize Liquidity:** One project is short of cash while another has excess funds. The Treasury Agent evaluates the below. It then recommends how liquidity can be redistributed to improve overall cash availability.  
            1. Upcoming obligations  
            2. Legal restrictions  
            3. Escrow rules  
            4. Inter-company funding policies  
         2.  **Recommend Bank Transfers:** The AI recommends transfers based on forecasted cash needs while respecting account restrictions and approval workflows.  
         3. **Recommend Fixed Deposit (FD) Investments:** The AI may recommend placing a portion of the surplus into approved short-term investments, considering:  
            1. Cash forecasts  
            2. Investment policy  
            3. Liquidity requirements  
            4. Expected returns  
         4. **Predict Liquidity Stress:** The AI continuously simulates future cash positions.  
         5. **Detect Treasury Risks:** The AI monitors for risks such as below. Each risk is prioritized based on severity and potential financial impact:  
            1. Excessive borrowing  
            2. Bank concentration  
            3. Idle cash  
            4. High FX exposure  
            5. Large debt maturities  
            6. Unused credit facilities  
            7. Unexpected cash movements  
         6. **Recommend Funding Source:** Suppose the company needs ₹100 Cr. Possible options:   
            1. Draw an existing construction loan  
            2. Use internal surplus cash  
            3. Raise equity  
            4. Arrange bridge finance  
            5. Refinance existing debt

            The AI compares these options based on the below factor. It presents a ranked set of recommendations for treasury to review:

            6. Cost of capital  
            7. Availability  
            8. Impact on liquidity  
            9. Covenants  
            10. Project timelines  
         7. **Recommend Hedge Strategy:** The AI analyzes:  
            1. Currency exposure  
            2. Market volatility  
            3. Existing hedge positions  
            4. Treasury policy  
         8. **Optimize Idle Cash:** The AI evaluates:  
            1. Upcoming cash needs  
            2. Loan interest rates  
            3. Investment options  
            4. Liquidity requirements
